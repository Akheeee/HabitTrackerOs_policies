Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and shared when you use our Habit Tracker Ös application ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you understand how we handle your data.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect:
    • Email address
    • Password (stored securely, hashed and encrypted)
  • Sign in with Apple: When you choose to authenticate with Apple:
    • Apple User ID
    • Email address (if you choose to share it)
    • Name (if you choose to share it)
  • Habit Data: Information you create within the App:

    • Habit completion logs and dates

  • Device Information: We may collect information about your iOS device, including device model, iOS version, app version, and device identifiers

Subscription and Payment Information

Note: We do not collect or store your credit card or payment information. All payments are processed securely by Apple.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  1. Providing Core Services: Account creation, storing habit data, sending notifications
  2. Subscription Management: Managing Pro features and in-app purchases
  3. App Improvement: Fixing bugs and improving performance
  4. Communication: Sending important updates and responding to support requests

Data Storage and Security

Local Storage

  • Your habit data is primarily stored locally on your device
  • We do not transmit your habit completion data to external servers

Security Measures

  • Passwords are hashed and encrypted
  • All API communications use HTTPS encryption
  • Device data is stored in iOS's secure sandbox environment

Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party service providers:

  • RevenueCat: Subscription management - Privacy Policy
  • Apple Services: Sign in with Apple, App Store, Push Notifications

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and Portability: View and export your data
  • Modification: Edit or update your information anytime
  • Deletion: Delete your account through Settings → Account → Delete Account
  • Notifications: Manage in iOS Settings
  • Subscription Management: Manage through iOS Settings

Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Data retained while your account is active
  • Deleted Accounts: All data permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Inactive Accounts: Accounts inactive for 3+ years may be deleted with notice

Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

California residents have additional rights including:

  • Right to know what personal data is collected

European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

EEA residents have rights under GDPR including:

  • Right to rectification
