Privacy Policy

Who We Are

Habit Tracker Ös ("the App") is developed and operated by Akhmed Mongush, an individual developer based in Argentina ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy. Contact: ar.akhmed.mongush@gmail.com.

This policy explains what information the App collects, why, where it is stored, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Account information

  • Email sign-in: your email address and password. Passwords are never stored in plain text; our authentication provider stores only a secure hash.
  • Sign in with Apple: an Apple user identifier and the email address Apple shares with us (this can be a private relay address). If you share your name, it is stored only on your device.
  • An internal account ID that links your data together.

Habit data you create

  • Habit names, emoji, colors, scheduled days, start dates and the "when" context you choose (for example "after breakfast")
  • Check-offs, rest days, streaks and progress
  • Your daily reminder time and any custom timings or contexts you create

Subscription information

  • Whether you have Pro, which plan, and its renewal or expiry date
  • Purchase history received from the App Store through RevenueCat
  • We never receive or store your card or payment details. All payments are handled by Apple.

Technical information

  • Our service providers automatically log basic technical data when the App connects to them, such as IP address, device model, iOS version, app version and request times. This is used for security, fraud prevention and fixing problems.
  • The App does not contain advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it does not access your contacts, photos, location or health data.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the App: creating your account, signing you in, saving and syncing your habits across your devices.
  • To send account emails: verification codes and password reset codes.
  • To manage subscriptions: unlocking Pro features and restoring purchases.
  • For reminders: your reminder time is used to schedule notifications. Reminders are local notifications created on your device; no push server is involved.
  • For support and security: answering your messages, preventing abuse and fixing bugs.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Where Your Data Is Stored

  • Your account and habit data are stored in a cloud database operated by Supabase, located in Canada, so your habits stay in sync and are not lost if you change or reset your device. A copy is also kept on your device.
  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and access to it is restricted to your own account.
  • Because our providers operate in other countries, your data may be processed outside your country of residence, including in Canada and the United States, with appropriate safeguards.

Service Providers We Use

We share data only with the providers we need to run the App, and only for that purpose:

  • Supabase: account sign-in and database hosting (Privacy Policy)
  • RevenueCat: subscription management (Privacy Policy)
  • Apple: App Store payments and Sign in with Apple (Privacy Policy)
  • Google (Gmail): delivery of verification and password reset emails (Privacy Policy)

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users.

Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and copy: email us to receive a copy of your data.
  • Correction: edit your habits in the App at any time, or ask us to correct account details.
  • Deletion: delete your account in the App under Settings → Delete Account, or ask us by email.
  • Notifications: turn reminders off in the App or in iOS Settings.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.

We respond to requests within 30 days.

Data Retention

  • We keep your data while your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, your account, habits, check-offs, settings and subscription records are deleted from our database immediately. Data on your device is removed when you delete the App.
  • We keep a minimal record that the deletion happened (account ID, email address, IP address and date) to show we completed your request, and delete it after 12 months.
  • Backups and logs held by our providers are overwritten on their regular cycle, generally within 30 days.
  • Accounts inactive for more than 3 years may be deleted after we notify you by email.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
